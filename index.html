The National Anthem of India is Jana-gana-mana.                            It was originally composed in Bengali by Rabindranath Tagore and was adopted in its Hindi version by the Constituent Assembly on January 24, 1950. The full version of the anthem takes approximately 52 seconds to play, while a short version (the first and last lines) is played on certain occasions and takes about 20 seconds.
